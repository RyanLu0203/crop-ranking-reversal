# Empirical claim boundary

## Admissible descriptive claims

- The parser recovers 345 official NASS crop-state-year rows including U.S. totals. The complete three-crop sample contains 231 rows, 77 state-years, and 26 states; 25 states remain in 2024 because New Jersey winter-wheat estimates were discontinued.
- Ranking discordance may be reported within that selected sample. Under standardized operating margin, 63 of 77 state-years have a top-rank discordance and 41 have a strong discordance; mean pairwise inversions are 1.792 of three.
- The result is definition-sensitive: top-rank discordance is 67/77 for relative yield, 48/77 for standardized revenue, 63/77 for operating margin, and 65/77 for total-cost margin.
- The national check is a retained null: national margin and planted-acreage ranks agree for all three crops in each of 2022, 2023, and 2024.
- The leakage-free 2024 relative-yield check has top-rank discordance in 22 of 25 states. It uses only two training years and is explicitly low-powered.

## Accounting-only claims

State yields combined with national ERS price/cost inputs can be used for transparent standardized accounting comparisons. Across operating-margin cells, observed-share value is on average 0.247 2024 dollars per admitted three-crop acre above equal shares and 74.738 below an all-share-on-top-score counterfactual. The latter ignores feasibility, rotation, price response, other crops, and adjustment costs; neither difference is a welfare or causal estimate.

## Prohibited claims

- Observed acreage is not a model optimum and does not reveal a representative farmer objective.
- Discordance does not establish CVaR, copula, risk aversion, suitability error, or private constraints as causes.
- National prices/costs do not measure state realized margins.
- Three annual observations do not identify state downside risk or empirical CVaR.
- The selected all-three-crop states are not representative of every U.S. state or farm.
- No county, farm, or national-universality claim is admissible.
