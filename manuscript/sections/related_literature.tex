\section{Related literature}

\paragraph{Prediction and prescription in agriculture.}
Agricultural machine learning commonly predicts yields or classifies agronomic outcomes from weather, soil, sensor and remote-sensing inputs \citep{liakos2018machine,vanklompenburg2020crop}. Such models can support a ranking, but they do not encode the feasible set or objective required for acreage prescription. The broader prescriptive-analytics literature evaluates predictions through downstream decisions and regret \citep{bertsimas2020predictive,elmachtoub2022smart}, while decision-focused learning differentiates or searches through optimization structure \citep{wilder2019melding,mandi2023decision}. Our analysis does not propose a new learning algorithm. It instead isolates the identification gap that must be resolved before a prediction can be interpreted as a land allocation.

\paragraph{Constrained crop planning.}
The closest included crop-planning studies optimize crop mix under stochastic returns, rotations or climate uncertainty. \citet{filippi2017mixed} combine realistic crop-selection constraints with a CVaR criterion; \citet{boyabatli2019crop} link allocation and rotation benefits dynamically; \citet{benini2023crop} formulate multi-period rotation and diversification constraints; and \citet{randall2022robust} study robustness across climate projections. The systematic review by \citet{randall2024systematic} documents the diversity of optimization approaches in the field. These studies motivate a multi-crop operational set, but they do not equate ordinal recommendations with the entire optimal solution set.

\paragraph{Downside risk and dependence.}
Loss-CVaR is convex and admits a finite-scenario auxiliary representation that remains valid with atoms \citep{rockafellar2000optimization,rockafellar2002conditional}. Copula models can separate marginal distributions from dependence, but tail properties vary by family \citep{demarta2005tcopula,ansari2024dependence}. We consequently condition any dependence comparison on a named family and a verified ordering of portfolio-loss distributions. This is stricter than treating a lower-tail coefficient as a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Information and operational flexibility.}
Stochastic programming separates here-and-now choices from actions taken after information arrives \citep{dantzig1955linear}. Information has operational value only when it changes feasible decisions \citep{keisler2004value,merkhofer1975value}; the value of flexible resources likewise depends on margins, costs and the complete uncertainty environment \citep{vanmieghem1998investment}. These results support non-negativity and weak action-set monotonicity, but not general strict complementarity between signal precision and flexibility.

Relative to this verified set, the present study differs by jointly comparing an ordinal crop ranking with an entire constrained loss-CVaR optimal set, separating possible, universal and selected reversals, and pairing that theory with a claim-bounded descriptive empirical audit. This is a bounded distinction, not a claim to be the first study of crop allocation, CVaR or prediction-informed decisions.
