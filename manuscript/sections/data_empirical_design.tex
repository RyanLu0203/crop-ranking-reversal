\section{Data and empirical design}

The empirical analysis uses the admitted official crop-production summary for state planted acreage and yield in \EmpiricalStartYear--\EmpiricalEndYear, together with admitted national price, cost and CPI series. We retain state-years with complete acreage and yield for corn, soybeans and winter wheat, without imputing published missing codes. The final panel contains \NCropRows{} crop rows, \NStateYears{} state-years and \NStates{} states. It is a complete-case aggregate panel, not a nationally representative sample of farms.

Four score definitions are frozen before comparison with acreage. Relative yield divides state yield by the same-year national yield. Standardized revenue multiplies state yield by the national same-year price. Operating margin subtracts national operating cost per planted acre, and total-cost margin subtracts national total cost. Monetary variables are expressed in real 2024 US dollars using CPI-U. These definitions are transparent accounting constructions: they are not local realized margins, farmer objectives or welfare measures.

For each state-year and definition, crops receive descending score ranks and planted-acreage shares. Concurrent pairwise inversion intensity is the fraction of informative crop pairs for which the score and acreage orders disagree; ties are retained explicitly. Top-rank reversal records whether a unique score leader is not the acreage leader. We use \BootstrapReplications{} deterministic state-cluster bootstrap draws for 95\% intervals, preserving within-state dependence across the three-year panel.

The temporal design creates two rolling-origin transitions per eligible state and score definition, giving 51 transitions per definition, \TransitionEvents{} rank-transition events and \CropTransitionRows{} crop-transition rows. Scores at $t-1$ are compared with acreage-share changes from $t-1$ to $t$. The top-minus-other contrast asks whether the previously top-scoring crop changes share differently from the other two crops. Prior acreage enters only as an inertia proxy. It is not labelled a rotation, capacity or adjustment constraint.

Aggregation is predeclared as an identification boundary. State-level results are compared with national crop-year ranks under the same definitions. Relative-yield national scores are tied by construction, so those comparisons are non-informative rather than treated as agreement. Two isolated raw-to-output executions reproduce all 19 empirical artifacts byte for byte. Source identifiers, timestamps, units, raw checksums, processing contracts and parameter scope are registered.

The admitted sources do not reveal private budgets, rotations, contracts, risk limits, beliefs or objectives. Observed acreage therefore cannot be certified as an optimum of the stochastic programme. The empirical estimands describe score--acreage order relations and transitions; they do not identify CVaR, copula causality, welfare or a private constraint mechanism.
