# GOAL-13 visualization audit

## Outcome

The Stage II figure system contains six main figure groups and four
supplementary figures. All ten have editable SVG and PDF exports, exact-size
300-dpi PNG and 600-dpi LZW TIFF exports, versioned tidy source data, upstream
lineage, captions, checksums, grayscale views and deuteranopia simulations.

## Promotion boundary

The main-positive simulation evidence is limited to E2 and E6. Figure 4
reports the passed E2 operational factorial and marks E3 as a failed-precision
adverse result. Figure 5 reports the passed E6 positive, null and substitution
patterns and marks E5 cross-law comparisons as failed-precision diagnostics.
Figure S2 retains the complete E1/E3/E4/E5 inventory and all 206 registered
infeasible rows. No failed experiment was filtered to create a positive claim.

## Visual inspection

The original ten-figure contact sheet was inspected at native resolution on
2026-07-22. Panel labels, axes, legends, uncertainty intervals, status labels
and boundaries are visible; no text or legend was observed outside its canvas.
The same source renders were checked in grayscale and a deuteranopia
simulation. Colour is redundant with labels, position, line style or explicit
status text.

## Scientific limits

KKT terms are labelled local pressures, not causal effects. M0–M4 and Shapley
outputs are closed synthetic-model decompositions. Figure 6 uses admitted
descriptive data only and does not infer private objectives, constraints,
copula laws, CVaR preferences, welfare or optimal acreage.

## Reproduction

Run `python scripts/generate_stage_ii_figures.py`, then
`python scripts/validate_stage_ii_visualization.py`. The validator checks all
exports, vector-native SVG text, dimensions, source/upstream hashes, evidence
boundaries, registry checksums, accessibility files and full infeasibility
accounting.
