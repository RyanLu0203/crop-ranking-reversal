# Reference reliability audit

## Status

The final bibliography contains 36 records: 31 scholarly/context records and
five official USDA/BLS data-source records. Scholarly metadata has been
checked against DOI registries, publisher pages, official proceedings, author
manuscripts or institutional repositories. The institutional records do not
have DOI identifiers and are instead verified by their official HTTPS URLs;
no placeholder DOI is invented. The detailed machine-readable record is
`literature_registry.csv`; claim support is separated in
`claim_reference_matrix.csv`.

## Search process

Google Scholar was used for discovery, related-article chaining and
forward-citation tracing across crop planning, agricultural CVaR/copulas,
joint crop tails, predict-then-optimize and climate-information value.
Publisher pages and Crossref were used for metadata. A Crossref batch request
returned HTTP 429, so affected records were verified individually on
Elsevier, Springer, Wiley, Emerald, Oxford Academic, AMS and DOAJ pages.

## Reliability classes

- Theory foundations support mathematical definitions or scope conditions.
- Canonical context supports domain positioning, not this paper's result.
- Original results are supported by repository proofs, official data or
  registered computation rather than external citations.
- The Mandi et al. decision-focused-learning review is explicitly labelled a
  preprint.

## Claims not transferred from sources

No reference is treated as proving the present reversal theorem, Kansas crop
shares, farm-level prevalence, causal effects or welfare. Publisher abstracts
do not supply numerical inputs. Google Scholar citation counts are recorded
only as changing discovery metadata.

## Unverified items

No citation used in the final manuscript is known to be nonexistent or
metadata-incomplete. Full-text access was not required for every peripheral
context source once DOI and claim scope were verified; such sources support
only broad positioning. USDA NASS annual reports, the USDA ERS Commodity Costs
and Returns product and the BLS CPI data page are treated as primary data
documentation, not as support for mechanism or causal claims. The author list
and manuscript affiliation are not a literature issue and require supervisor
confirmation separately.
