## Remaining scientific limitations

The public panel is aggregate and uses national price and cost inputs. It does
not observe private beliefs, land quality, liquidity, rotations, contracts,
insurance, downside-risk limits or the action set considered by a farm. The
controlled parameters do not estimate field prevalence. No causal, welfare,
regret, private-optimality, county-validation or universal dependence claim is
made. Author metadata and journal-specific formatting remain submission tasks.
