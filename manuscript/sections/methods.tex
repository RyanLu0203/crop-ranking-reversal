\section{Methods}

\subsection{Theory and optimization}
Every preserved theorem was classified before implementation. The canonical model uses portfolio loss rather than the favourable profit tail, permits general positive finite-scenario weights, leaves the VaR auxiliary variable free and retains land, budget, shared-constraint and bound multipliers. Possible and universal reversal are computed by minimizing and maximizing pairwise acreage gaps over the objective-equivalent optimal face. Equality, feasibility and complementarity checks use frozen tolerances.

\subsection{Confirmatory design and computation}
The six-experiment design was frozen before execution. Each experiment specifies factors, estimands, multiplicity, sequential replication checks, precision targets and a ceiling. E2 crosses operational interventions against a fixed ranking and law. E6 crosses signal information and feasible-action flexibility in three constructive environments. E1, E3, E4 and E5 test margin, risk-frontier and dependence questions. Scenario seeds, laws and hashes are registered. Each solve records status, objective, allocation, loss-CVaR, named slacks, binding flags, duals, tail weights, KKT residuals and optimal-face gaps.

Family-wise intervals use the coverage and multiplicity frozen for each experiment. Promotion requires every registered primary interval to pass at the experiment level; isolated passing contrasts inside a failed experiment remain adverse. Registered infeasible rows are preserved and classified, never discarded to form a positive subset. Reverse-order replay reconstructs selected tasks, and solver sensitivity compares HiGHS methods at registered anchors.

\subsection{Mechanism attribution}
The M0--M4 path adds registered blocks to a closed benchmark. Because sequential contributions depend on order, all subset coalitions are solved and Shapley contributions are computed for allocation, expected profit, loss-CVaR and concentration. The efficiency residual compares the sum of contributions with the full-minus-base difference. KKT pressure terms are evaluated locally from Eq.~\ref{eq:kktpair}; they are labelled accounting terms, not causal acreage effects.

\subsection{Official data and empirical estimands}
Raw sources enter only through a registry containing authority, stable identifier, retrieval date, geography, time support, units and checksum. The parser applies a complete three-crop rule. National prices and costs are joined after crop-year reconciliation and converted to real 2024 dollars. Four ranking definitions are computed without post-result selection.

Concurrent inversion intensity uses informative pairwise comparisons within state-year. Cluster intervals resample states with replacement for \BootstrapReplications{} deterministic draws. Rolling-origin contrasts use the previous year's score status and the subsequent change in planted-acreage share; state clustering preserves serial and cross-crop dependence. National aggregation repeats the ranking construction and marks tied definitions non-informative. All empirical claims are descriptive or accounting-identified.

\subsection{Visualization, reproducibility and claim governance}
Figure scripts read only verified theory, simulation and empirical outputs. The frozen colour card is \texttt{\#3D3539}, \texttt{\#0F9EA8}, \texttt{\#008B82}, \texttt{\#45728F}, \texttt{\#8CD1B2} and \texttt{\#8B84A3}; colour is redundant with text, position, marker or line style. Every figure has SVG, PDF, 300-dpi PNG and 600-dpi TIFF exports, tidy source data, checksums, grayscale proofs and deuteranopia simulations.

Manuscript numbers are generated from verified outputs and registered at field level. Claim--citation and figure-use registries link prose to theory, simulation, empirical evidence and source data. The paper and Supplementary Information are compiled twice in isolated directories with a fixed source date; byte identity, embedded fonts, page rendering, references and checksums are validated before packaging.
