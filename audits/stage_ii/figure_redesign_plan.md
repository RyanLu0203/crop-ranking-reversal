# GOAL-13 Nature-level figure redesign plan

## Design principle

Stage II figures are visual arguments, not restyled Stage I charts. Every main
figure has one question, one sentence conclusion, a dominant evidence panel,
registered source data and a blocking evidence gate. `figure_blueprint.csv` is
the canonical contract. GOAL-13 must not draw a panel whose gate is still
blocked.

The six groups follow the paper's evidence ladder:

1. decision architecture;
2. geometry of reversal;
3. controlled mechanism decomposition;
4. reversal and actionability regimes;
5. diversification failure and misspecification regret;
6. model-linked empirical validation.

The existing Stage I conceptual and empirical figures are source material, not
fixed layouts. The current Figure 1 is boundary-led and visually gives equal
weight to every step. The Stage II version should foreground the decision object
and show exactly which inputs are observed, assumed or latent. The current Figure
2 is a strong descriptive audit, but its 26-row heatmap is too dominant for a
mechanism paper and its three-year cell rates convey more visual certainty than
the denominators warrant. Its information should move into the richer Figure 6
or Extended Data with uncertainty and temporal depth.

## Backend and export contract

The canonical repository already uses Python/matplotlib exclusively; GOAL-13
continues that backend for generation, preview, export and QA. Switching backend
would require an explicit versioned decision before any figure is generated.

- Main size: 183 mm double-column unless a figure demonstrably reads at 89 mm;
  maximum height 170 mm including space planning for the legend.
- Text: editable Arial/Helvetica-compatible sans serif, 5--7 pt at final size;
  lowercase bold 8-pt panel labels.
- Vector: editable PDF and plain SVG with TrueType 42 text; raster preview only
  for review. Keep RGB and embedded components.
- Axes: labelled units, ticks, no decorative background grid, no coloured text.
- Colour: accessible neutral/signal/accent system; red/green is never the only
  distinction; grayscale and colour-vision simulation must pass.
- Layout: one hero panel plus subordinate evidence; panels sized by evidence
  importance, alphabetic and space-efficient.
- Legends: define exact n, replication unit, center, interval/error bar, test or
  descriptive status, correction, model/data law and source-data file.

These constraints reflect Nature's figure guide and initial-submission guidance
accessed 2026-07-21:

- https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/
- https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/building-and-exporting-figure-panels/
- https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/initial-submission

## Figure 1 — Decision architecture

**Core conclusion:** rankings require cardinal, stochastic, feasibility and
selection information before they imply an allocation.

- Hero: a left-to-right decision architecture with input status encoded as
  observed, estimated, design-controlled or latent.
- Support: solution-set reversal definitions and the five research questions.
- Unique job: establish the vocabulary used by every later figure.
- Avoid: empirical counts, decorative crop icons, and arrows that imply observed
  acreage was generated by the model.

## Figure 2 — Geometry of reversal

**Core conclusion:** margin, operational and risk mechanisms move or truncate the
optimal face in geometrically distinct ways.

- Hero: feasible region, ranking half-space, objective direction, risk boundary
  and optimal face in a validated low-dimensional case.
- Support: matched margin-only, constraint-forced, risk-induced and set-valued
  examples.
- Unique job: make possible/universal/selected reversal and face uncertainty
  visually unavoidable.
- Avoid: using a convenient selected vertex as the whole optimum.

## Figure 3 — Nested mechanism decomposition

**Core conclusion:** M0--M4 additions create measurable and auditable changes in
acreage, expected profit, true-law CVaR and local optimality pressure.

- Hero: nested allocation transition using controlled confirmatory contrasts.
- Support: profit/CVaR trade-off, named active constraints/duals and attribution
  intervals over selection/order.
- Unique job: supply positive quantitative mechanism evidence.
- Avoid: interpreting a block waterfall as unique causal attribution.

## Figure 4 — Regimes and information actionability

**Core conclusion:** flexibility changes both reversal regimes and whether signal
precision can alter feasible action.

- Hero: fixed-factor dependence by risk-tolerance regime map, faceted by
  operational flexibility.
- Support: signal-precision by flexibility VOI surface, actionability boundary,
  and at least one null or substitution case.
- Unique job: connect downside-risk and information questions through feasible
  action space.
- Avoid: smoothing across active-set discontinuities or calling a crossing a
  universal threshold.

## Figure 5 — Diversification failure

**Core conclusion:** a portfolio can appear diversified by variance while
concentrating true-tail loss or incurring regret under dependence
misspecification.

- Hero: true-law and assumed-law risk--return frontiers with selected policies.
- Support: tail-contribution decomposition, concentration/support, and paired
  true-law regret.
- Unique job: replace the false Draft pseudo-diversification proposition with a
  positive, decision-relevant and bounded result.
- Avoid: treating crop count, low correlation or the diagnostic flag as welfare.

## Figure 6 — Model-linked empirical validation

**Core conclusion:** longer-horizon pre-decision rankings and subsequent acreage
transitions display robust, heterogeneous patterns that can be compared with
named model predictions without revealing private optimality.

- Hero: continuous score/rank transition versus next-period acreage-share change.
- Support: uncertainty-aware geographic heterogeneity, rolling/geographic
  holdout performance, aggregation sensitivity and the national null.
- Unique job: close the theory--simulation--observation loop.
- Avoid: mapping simulation shadow prices or mechanism labels directly onto
  observed acreage.

## Extended Data and Supplementary allocation

Main figures are limited to the decisive evidence. The following belong in
Extended Data or Supplementary Information:

- sample flow, missingness, data-vintage and geography coverage;
- complete alternative-ranking heatmaps and crop-set sensitivity;
- convergence, stopping, replay, solver and optimal-face diagnostics;
- family-specific dependence sweeps and all null/multiple crossing sets;
- full nested-model anchor inventory and attribution-order sensitivity;
- alternative diversification definitions and negative controls;
- information substitution/counterexample cases;
- empirical robustness, placebo timing and aggregation tables.

Nature allows up to ten Extended Data display items, but the project should use
only the items required to audit the main claims.

## Source-data and QA gate

Every quantitative panel requires a tidy CSV/Parquet extract whose rows reproduce
the plotted marks, a lineage record to raw simulation/empirical output, a
generation command and a checksum. The validator must enforce:

- no manual plot data;
- no figure before its evidence gate passes;
- exact n and interval definitions in captions;
- selectable text and embedded fonts;
- final-size label and overlap checks;
- vector element counts and absence of rasterized chart text;
- grayscale and colour-vision accessibility;
- a visual-regression baseline created only after scientific approval; and
- page-level inspection in the rebuilt manuscript.
