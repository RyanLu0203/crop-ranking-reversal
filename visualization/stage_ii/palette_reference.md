# Stage II colour-card reference

The user-supplied journal-style colour card received on 2026-07-22 is the
authoritative palette for the Stage II figures and final manuscript package.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Text / exact null | `#3D3539` | axes, text and exact-null evidence |
| Bright cyan | `#0F9EA8` | third crop and secondary accent |
| Deep teal | `#008B82` | supported positive evidence and second crop |
| Slate blue | `#45728F` | primary quantitative series and first crop |
| Mint | `#8CD1B2` | supported-status fields and light positive emphasis |
| Lavender | `#8B84A3` | secondary series and substitution evidence |

Light panel fields may use low-saturation tints derived from the card. Neutral
grey remains reserved for failed, adverse and unidentified evidence so that the
promotion boundary is not confused with a positive mechanism. Colour never
carries meaning alone: every status is also encoded by position, line style,
shape or explicit text. Grayscale and deuteranopia QA remain mandatory.
