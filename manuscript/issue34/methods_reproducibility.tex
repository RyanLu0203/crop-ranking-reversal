\section{Methods, data and reproducibility}

\subsection{Official sources and transformations}

State acreage and yield are parsed from registered USDA NASS annual reports
\citep{usdanass2019crop,usdanass2022crop,usdanass2025crop}.
National crop yield, harvest price and operating and total cost series are
from USDA NASS and ERS \citep{usdaers2026costs}. BLS annual CPI-U converts
monetary values to 2024 dollars \citep{bls2026cpi}. Raw reports are retained
unchanged with SHA-256 checksums. The
canonical crop-year and extended state-crop panels are produced by scripted
parsers and complete-case joins. No missing or suppressed agricultural value
is imputed.

The Kansas score uses only 2016--2023. For each crop and year, state yield is
divided by the national yield of the same crop; the score is the eight-year
mean. Margin is state yield times national harvest price minus national
operating cost, then expressed in real 2024 dollars. Student-\(t\), Gaussian
and empirical-quantile marginals are calibrated to the historical means and
dispersion. The primary score intentionally does not use the 2024 evaluation
year.

\subsection{Scenario generation and optimization}

Common random numbers are used within each model contrast. Primary
optimization uses 512 equally weighted scenarios and seed 202607270034.
Copula correlation is derived from the matched Kendall's \(\tau\). The
Student-\(t\) copula uses four degrees of freedom and Student-\(t\) marginals
five. Optimization is performed with SciPy/HiGHS through a dedicated solver
layer. Every solution is re-evaluated from scenario profit, resource use and
constraint violation, and the primal--dual certificate is checked.

Minimum-CVaR and expected-profit endpoints are solved separately for each
joint law. Equation~\ref{eq:riskpath} produces eleven risk ceilings. The
optimal-face audit constrains expected profit to its optimal value within
tolerance and solves all three ranked-pair min--max problems plus complete-rank
and exclusion feasibility checks. A deterministic HiGHS solution is reported
for selected reversal; universal and possible classifications use the face
bounds. Ordering and zero tolerances are \(10^{-4}\) normalized land share,
with sensitivity from \(10^{-8}\) to \(10^{-2}\).

\subsection{Policy and robustness design}

Score-proportional, score-winner and equal-share allocations are repaired to
operational feasibility before outcome comparison. Expected-profit,
mean--variance, full loss-CVaR and minimum-CVaR endpoints are solved from the
same constraints. Robustness varies one dimension at a time: confidence level,
dependence family and parameter, marginal model, historical window, scenario
count, seed, solver and removal of each constraint group. Null,
nonmonotone, multiple-optimum and infeasible cells are retained.

The risk-isolation path preserves the official Soybean--Corn score and mean
orders, then applies a 10\% mean-preserving severe soybean downside event equal
to one-third of its historical real gross revenue. The operational path fixes
the margin scenarios and a provably nonbinding risk ceiling, adds the
registered constraint sequence and then tightens only the soybean rotation
cap. These are repository-registered structural stresses, not externally
preregistered experiments or estimated private constraints.

Historical uncertainty resamples calibration years 64 times. Each replication
reconstructs scores and margins, regenerates scenarios and resolves the
frontier. Continuous outputs use historical-resample percentile intervals.
Binary reversal frequencies use exact Clopper--Pearson binomial intervals and
are labelled accordingly. Neither treats simulated scenarios as independent
empirical observations. The small number of
historical years is visible in every relevant figure and table.

\subsection{Information--flexibility programme}

The binary state is constructed from the corn-minus-soybean relative-yield
advantage around its historical median. Signal accuracy ranges from 0.5 to
1.0. A feasible constant policy is always available, which provides an exact
signal-ignorability check. The acreage-recourse path changes the share
adjustable after signal arrival. The buffering path contracts state-dependent
margins toward their common mean while retaining signal-contingent actions.
One loss-CVaR ceiling is applied to the full ex-ante state--signal loss
distribution. Numerical information values below solver tolerance are
classified as zero. For each adjacent \(q_1<q_2\), \(\phi_1<\phi_2\)
rectangle, interaction is the solved cross-difference
\([V(q_2,\phi_2)-V(q_1,\phi_2)]-[V(q_2,\phi_1)-V(q_1,\phi_1)]\);
the registered tolerance is \(10^{-7}\).

\subsection{External evidence}

For the 31-state panel, pairwise inversion intensity counts discordant pairs
among the three crops. Top-rank disagreement equals one when the highest score
crop is not the largest-acreage crop. Confidence intervals use state-cluster
resampling. The lagged model predicts next-year acreage-share change from the
previous relative-yield leader and previous acreage share with crop, state and
decision-year fixed effects. It is descriptive and uses no contemporaneous
score to predict the same year's acreage.

\subsection{Software and reproducibility}

The repository records the immutable design, raw checksums, processed data,
source tables, figures and manuscript numbers. The main reconstruction is
run with:
\begin{verbatim}
uv run python scripts/run_issue34_reconstruction.py
uv run python scripts/render_issue34_manuscript_numbers.py
uv run python scripts/make_issue34_figures.py
uv run pytest -q
\end{verbatim}
The generated output manifest and \texttt{SHA256SUMS.txt} must verify before
the manuscript is built. Figure text is editable in PDF/SVG; PNG and 600-dpi
TIFF are secondary exports. Full mathematical proofs, additional phase tables,
data dictionaries and validation results are supplied in Supplementary
Information and the reproducibility archive.
