## Inclusion boundary

Included work supports one of five roles: loss-CVaR foundations; joint-tail and
copula dependence; crop-planning operations; prediction-to-prescription; or
information value under actionable flexibility. No source is cited as proving
the present reversal theorem, the Kansas numerical result, causal field
prevalence or welfare effects.

## Reliability notes

- The eight-year Kansas calibration is too short for reliable extreme-tail
  estimation. Copula families are therefore registered stress paths.
- Publisher abstracts support methodological positioning, not quantitative
  transfer of results.
- Google Scholar citation counts are discovery metadata and may change.
- The Mandi et al. decision-focused-learning survey is explicitly a preprint.
